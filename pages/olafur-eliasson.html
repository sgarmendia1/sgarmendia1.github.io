Perception and environment

Olafur Eliasson's large-scale, often immersive installations use light, water, and natural phenomena to shape perception and highlight environmental concerns.

Themes: perception, environment, public art

Significance

  • Creates experiential works that invite public participation and reflection.
  • Brings attention to climate and environmental questions through art.
  • Known for museum installations and public commissions that transform space.

Art and activism

Ai Weiwei is a Chinese artist and activist whose installations, sculpture and social projects have engaged global audiences and questioned power, human rights, and freedom of expression.

Themes: activism, installation, documentary

Significance

  • Combines traditional craft and large-scale installations to critique political systems.
  • Uses social media and documentary practices to reach worldwide audiences.
  • His work blurred lines between artist and public intellectual in the 21st century.

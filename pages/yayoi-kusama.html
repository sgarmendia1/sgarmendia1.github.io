Infinity and repetition

Yayoi Kusama's mirrored rooms, polka dots, and large installations explore infinity, repetition, and the interplay of obsession and beauty.

Themes: installation, spectacle, mental health

Significance

  • Popularized immersive "Infinity Rooms" attracting global audiences.
  • Her work connects personal experience with universal sensory perception.
  • Has influenced museum programming and experiential art in the 21st century.

Digital art meets the marketplace

Beeple is a digital artist whose works helped popularize NFT sales and brought questions about digital ownership, circulation, and valuation into mainstream art discourse.

Themes: digital art, NFTs, circulation

Significance

  • Helped catalyze mainstream attention to NFTs and crypto art markets.
  • Raised debates about value, authorship, and the environmental impact of blockchain.
